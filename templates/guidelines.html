DISASTER SAFETY

Safety Guidelines

Simple steps to help you stay safe before, during, and after a disaster.

✓

Do's

Stay prepared and act safely.

  • ✓ Stay calm.
  • ✓ Follow official alerts.
  • ✓ Keep an emergency kit ready.
  • ✓ Keep your phone charged.
  • ✓ Move to a safe area.
  • ✓ Stay connected with family.
  • ✓ Keep emergency numbers handy.
  • ✓ Help those who need assistance.
  • ✓ Protect important documents.
  • ✓ Wait for the official all-clear.
!

Avoid

Stay away from unnecessary risks.

  • ! Panic and rushing.
  • ! Ignoring warnings.
  • ! Sharing unverified news.
  • ! Entering damaged buildings.
  • ! Touching fallen wires.
  • ! Blocking emergency routes.
  • ! Wasting essential supplies.
  • ! Returning too early.
  • ! Using elevators during evacuation.
  • ! Taking unnecessary risks.
i

Always follow instructions from local authorities and emergency services during an actual disaster.
